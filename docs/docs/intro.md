# Introduction

**Mealmate** is a shared recipe book, weekly meal planner, and shopping list app built for two people.

## What it does

- **Recipe book** — add and categorize recipes with ingredients and step-by-step instructions
- **Weekly meal planner** — plan breakfast, lunch, and dinner for each day of the week
- **Shopping list** — auto-generate a shopping list from the week's menu, with smart unit combining (500g + 1kg = 1.5kg)
- **Attribution** — see who added each item to the shopping list

## Who it's for

Two users (household partners) with shared data and individual logins.
